Privacy Policy

Pipe Drain · Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version

Pipe Drain collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, and nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The game stores the following on your device only:

  • Your progress: levels cleared, stars, gems, daily streak and unlocked skins
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, liquid symbols and confirm-before-spending
  • A puzzle you left halfway, so you can pick it up again
  • A record of how you play (levels started and finished, buttons used), kept in a file in the app's own storage. It is never sent anywhere and we cannot see it. It is tied to a random number created when the app is installed, not to you or your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We keep no copy and cannot recover it.

Advertising

None. The app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only asks Apple which purchases your account owns, so it can restore them.

Children

Pipe Drain is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
