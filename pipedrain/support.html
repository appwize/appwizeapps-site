Common questions

How do the pipes work?
Some tubes have a one-way pipe underneath. When a pipe tube is full but still holds more than one color, its top layer drains into the tube the pipe leads to, for free. That tube can be a pipe tube too, so one pour can set off a chain. Pipes start in the Cascade levels.
I'm stuck on a level.
Undo takes back a pour and Restart starts the level over, both free. A hint costs gems and shows a pour that leads to a solution; once bought, you can look at it again for free until you make that pour. Every level has been checked by a solver, so every level can be finished within its pour limit.
I bought Collector's Skins on another device, or reinstalled the app.
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so there is nothing to buy again. Gems are different: they are used up as you spend them and live only on the device, so gems (bought or earned) do not come back after deleting the app.
The Shop says “Store unavailable”.
The App Store could not be reached. It usually resolves on its own; if it does not, check your connection and reopen the app.
Some colors are hard to tell apart.
Turn on Liquid symbols in Settings: every liquid gets its own shape, so color is never the only difference. It is on automatically if Differentiate Without Color is on in the iPhone's accessibility settings. The game also works with VoiceOver.
I've finished every level. What now?
Endless mode keeps going with new puzzles, and there is a new Daily Challenge every day at midnight, your local time. You can also go back for three stars on any level.
Is my progress backed up?
No. Everything is stored on the device and nothing is sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes your progress along with it.
